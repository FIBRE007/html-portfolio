A Warm Welcome

Where Every Child is Known, Loved and Celebrated

Starting school is one of life's biggest adventures. At Royal Family Academy Junior School, we walk alongside every family from the very first day, creating a nurturing environment where children feel safe to explore, ask questions, and discover the joy of learning.

Our teachers don't just instruct — they disciple, encourage and champion each child's unique gifts, laying a foundation of faith, character and academic confidence that lasts a lifetime.

  • Low student-to-teacher ratios
  • Play-based & inquiry-led learning
  • Daily devotion & character coaching

Our Stages

A Learning Journey Built for Every Age

Each stage is thoughtfully designed around how children learn best at that age — always warm, always purposeful.

Ages 2 – 3

Early Years

Gentle first steps into structured play, sensory discovery and social bonding.

Ages 3 – 4

Nursery

Building language, motor skills and early numeracy through joyful, hands-on play.

Ages 4 – 5

Kindergarten

Foundational literacy, numeracy and independence to prepare for Primary school.

Ages 6 – 11

Primary

A rich, rigorous curriculum spanning Years 1 – 6, building scholars and leaders.

Curriculum

Learning That's Rich, Playful & Rigorous

Our curriculum blends the best of international early-years frameworks with a distinctly Christian, Nigerian heart — balancing literacy and numeracy mastery with the arts, physical development, and character education.

  • Phonics-based literacy programme
  • Hands-on STEM & creative arts
  • French & Yoruba language exposure

Our Learning Approach

Learning by Doing, Asking & Discovering

We believe young children learn best through play, exploration and real experiences. Our teachers design inquiry-led activities that build critical thinking while nurturing wonder, kindness and a love for God.

  • Montessori-inspired learning corners
  • Weekly outdoor & nature exploration
  • Continuous, gentle assessment
