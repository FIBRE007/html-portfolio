A Message From Our Principal

“At Royal Family Academy High School, we hold two convictions in equal measure: that every student can achieve academic excellence, and that true success is inseparable from character, faith and service. Our students are challenged to think critically, lead courageously, and walk in integrity — preparing them not merely to pass examinations, but to shape the world for good.”

Academics

A Curriculum Built for University & Beyond

Our secondary curriculum combines WAEC/NECO and Cambridge-aligned pathways with a strong emphasis on critical thinking, research and applied problem-solving — equipping students for competitive university admission worldwide.

  • Junior & Senior Secondary, plus Sixth Form
  • Science, Arts & Commercial tracks
  • Weekly study skills & mentorship

Beyond the Classroom

Where Leaders Are Shaped

Science, Technology, Engineering & Maths

Fully equipped physics, chemistry, biology and computer science labs, robotics and coding clubs, and an annual STEM Innovation Fair that has produced regional award-winning projects.

Music, Drama & Visual Arts

From the school choir and orchestra to termly drama productions and a dedicated art studio, students discover and refine creative gifts alongside their academics.

Sports & Athletics

Football, basketball, athletics, swimming and table tennis compete in inter-house and interschool leagues, building discipline, resilience and teamwork.

Leadership Development

Every student is invited into leadership — from class representatives and house captains to the Student Council and Head Prefect, mentored by staff advisors throughout.

Clubs & Societies

Debate, Model United Nations, Christian Union, Press Club, Entrepreneurship Society and more — over 20 clubs for students to explore their passions.

University Preparation

Dedicated university counsellors guide students from Year 9 through applications — SAT/UCAS preparation, personal statements, and scholarship guidance for destinations worldwide.
