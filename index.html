The Book

The Life of Grace

Living from the Fourth Dimension

A biblical guide to seeing, thinking, speaking and living from God's perspective. Across a Preface and eleven chapters, it traces a single argument — from the reality of the unseen realm, through the finished work of Christ and the believer's new identity, to a renewed mind, a living faith, and a life of thanksgiving, peace and rest that shines as a son sent into the world.

By Fadoju Tosin · Published by The Assembly of Sons
