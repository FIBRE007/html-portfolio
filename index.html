Our Books

Read & Listen

Two books from The Assembly of Sons, each unfolding a different facet of the same message: the sufficiency of grace, received through faith in Christ.

Book One

The Life of Grace

Living from the Fourth Dimension

A biblical guide to seeing, thinking, speaking and living from God's perspective — from the reality of the unseen realm to the finished work of Christ, a renewed mind and a life that shines as a son sent into the world.

Book Two

The Psychology of Grace Through Faith

Working From Grace, Not for Grace

A practical exploration of what changes when grace shapes not only salvation, but the way we think, work, expect, manage resources, relate to people and respond to setbacks.
