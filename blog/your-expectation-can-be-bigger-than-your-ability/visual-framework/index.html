Start with what God has given you reason to believe. Then look honestly at what is in front of you, use faithfully what is in your hands, and trust God beyond the boundary of your own ability. Tap each stage to open it.

01
Expectation begins hereWhat has God given me reason to believe?
Faith does not begin by asking how impressive your ability is. It begins with who God is, what He has said, and what He has genuinely given you reason to expect. Your desire does not become a promise simply because you want it strongly.
02
See reality honestlyWhat do I see right now?
Your circumstances are real and worth understanding. But they are information about your present situation—not a measurement of God's ability. You do not have to deny the facts in order to refuse to make them God.
03
Recognise the channelsWhat is presently in my hands?
Receive and steward what is available to you. These things matter, but they are means through which help or provision may come. They are not the Source.

04
ParticipationWhat is mine to do?
Trust does not make you passive. Do responsibly what is within your hands while trusting God concerning what is beyond them.

05
The human boundaryWhere does my ability end?
There are outcomes you cannot produce, people you cannot control, doors you cannot force open and circumstances you cannot guarantee. Reaching that boundary does not mean you have reached the boundary of God.

Beyond the human boundary

God's ability does not end here.

God remains the Source even when a channel changes, closes or reaches its limit.

GOD — SOURCE

The One in whom confidence ultimately rests. His ability is not measured by the resources presently visible to you.

CHANNELS

People · money · jobs · medicine · opportunities · skills. Receive them gratefully. Use them faithfully. Do not confuse them with God.

I do what is responsibly within my hands.

I trust God with what is beyond my hands.

“The present channel may change. My Source has not.”
