Stop Trying to Earn Grace

Understanding What It Means to Work From Grace, Not for Grace

By Fadoju Tosin

← All articles

There is something many sincere Christians do without realizing it. We receive salvation as a gift, but somewhere along the journey, we begin trying to earn everything else from God.

Think about how you came to Christ. You did not clean yourself up first and then present yourself to God as someone worthy of saving. You came because you needed a Saviour. And God did not respond to your need by giving you a list of things to accomplish. He gave you Christ.

Scripture says:

“For by grace you have been saved through faith... it is the gift of God, not of works.”Ephesians 2:8–9 (WEB)

That is grace in its clearest form: God provides; faith receives.

But strangely, after beginning this way, we can start relating to God differently. We pray, fast, give, serve and try to live right—and these are good things. The problem begins when, in our hearts, they become the reason we expect God to be good to us.

Then when something goes wrong, the questions begin.

Did I not pray enough? Is God disappointed with me? Have I done something that has made Him withdraw His help?

Before long, our relationship with God becomes a constant attempt to qualify.

That is not the rest grace gives.

Grace does not mean that how we live no longer matters. It means that our obedience is a response to God's grace, not an attempt to purchase it.

Paul understood this. He said:

“But by the grace of God I am what I am... I worked more than all of them; yet not I, but the grace of God which was with me.”1 Corinthians 15:10 (WEB)

Notice that Paul did not stop working because of grace. He worked from grace.

That distinction matters.

You can pray desperately, hoping your prayer will finally persuade God to help you. Or you can pray confidently because God has already invited you to “draw near with boldness to the throne of grace” to receive mercy and find grace in time of need (Hebrews 4:16).

The prayer may sound similar, but the heart is standing in a completely different place.

Conclusion

Perhaps this is the truth your heart needs to settle into today: the God who gave you Christ before you could earn salvation has not suddenly become a God whose goodness must be purchased by your performance.

So pray. Obey. Serve. Give. Work hard. Repent when you are wrong and learn when you fail.

But do it all from grace.

You are not trying to make God gracious. You are responding to the God who has already revealed Himself as gracious in Jesus Christ.

That is where the Christian life begins.

And that is where we must learn to live.

Adapted from The Psychology of Grace Through Faith: Working From Grace, Not for Grace
Fadoju Tosin

Scripture quotations are from the World English Bible (WEB).
